Benkyo Gate: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary

Benkyo Gate does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything it stores stays on your own device.

What the extension stores locally

Using Chrome's storage.local on your computer, the extension saves:

  • Your settings (quiz price, vocab/conjugation mix, and any custom words you add).
  • Today's count of unlocked videos and the IDs of videos you've unlocked today, so you aren't charged twice for the same video. This resets every day.
  • A tally of which practice items you've missed, so they come up more often.

This data never leaves your browser. It is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

Website access

The extension runs only on youtube.com pages, where it pauses videos and shows the practice quiz. It does not read your watch history, account details, comments, or any other page content, and it makes no network requests.

Third parties

There are no analytics, ads, trackers, or remote code. The optional Venmo donation link opens Venmo's website, which has its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions? Use the support link on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
